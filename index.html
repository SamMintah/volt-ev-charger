FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still have a question? Email us

When can I download Volt?

We're launching in Accra first, then Kumasi and Takoradi. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to get access.

Which cars does Volt work with?

Any electric car with a CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO or GB/T connector. Add your car and Volt shows only chargers that fit it.

How do I pay?

With MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, Visa or Mastercard. For MoMo you approve each payment on your phone with your PIN; Volt never sees it.

Does Volt charge extra?

You pay the station's price per kWh plus a small, clearly shown service fee. No subscriptions.

I run chargers. How do I join?

Use the operator form above. We can list your sites right away and connect live status and payments when you're ready.
